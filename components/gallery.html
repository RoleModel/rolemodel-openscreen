Video components

Custom elements for HyperFrames scenes. Colour comes from Optics; nothing here hard-codes a hex. Every component takes at (ms it appears) and for (ms it stays). Drag the scrubber — the whole page is seeked to that instant, which is exactly what the renderer does frame by frame.
